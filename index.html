🩺 General Clinical Guidance

A General Medicine clinician may be a reasonable first step

Stomach pain can have multiple causes, and this AI assistant does not provide a definitive diagnosis. If you experience severe or worsening pain, continuous vomiting, or high fever, seek emergency care immediately. Otherwise, a qualified clinician at a Primary Health Centre (PHC) can assess your condition.

⚠️
Red Flag Symptoms: Severe sudden pain, continuous vomiting, breathlessness, or black stool require urgent in-person medical evaluation.
